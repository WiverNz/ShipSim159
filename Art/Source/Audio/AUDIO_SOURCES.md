# ShipSimulator audio library: sources and licenses

Generated by `build_ship_audio.py --build` from its registry; edit the script, not this file. Game files are in `Assets/ShipSimulator/Audio/Library/`, shared by every vessel profile.

No recording here is of any catalogue vessel, of its engines or of its deck machinery. "Analogue recording" means a recording of comparable equipment used as a substitute; "recording of the phenomenon" is wind recorded in the open; "synthesized approximation" is original synthesis by this project, released with the project. Every source below is CC0 1.0: no attribution is required, and the authors are credited as a courtesy.
